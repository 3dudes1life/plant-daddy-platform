# Free vs. Ultra

## Free

Plant Daddy Free is a real product, not a crippled trial.

### Phone-only

- add/name plants
- sites/zones
- care profiles
- ideal ranges
- manual watering/fertilizer/repotting/events
- notes/photos/history
- reminders
- manual moisture readings

### Supported sensor connected

- automatic raw readings
- basic history/charts
- thresholds
- dry alerts
- device battery/signal/health

**Raw customer data is not paywalled.**

## Ultra — target $1.99/month

Ultra sells intelligence + convenience + automation:

- plant-specific baseline learning
- drying-rate interpretation
- time-to-watering prediction
- weather-aware recommendations
- advanced anomaly alerts
- approval workflows
- supported smart irrigation
- bounded automatic watering
- natural-language caretaker explanations

## Upsell rule

Do not create fear. Surface genuine value moments.

Good:

> Daddy noticed this plant is drying much faster than its normal pattern.

Bad:

> Upgrade or your plant may die.
